# Multi-Factor Signal Research Engine

This document details the architecture, mathematical formulations, evidence extraction groups, conflict detection, score conservation, and analytical policies of the **Multi-Factor Signal Research Engine** implemented in **Phase 5** of the **Crypto AI Trading Intelligence Platform**.

---

## 1. Research Scope & Safety Boundaries

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Research Disclaimer**:
> All signals generated by this engine (`LONG_SETUP`, `SHORT_SETUP`, `NEUTRAL`, `WAIT`) are **analytical research classifications**, NOT guaranteed forecasts, financial advice, or trade execution orders.
> - **Zero Execution Capabilities**: No automated trading, broker execution, paper trading, position sizing, stop losses, or take profits.
> - **Zero Probabilistic Win Claims**: Scores (e.g. $+51.1$) represent net directional evidence on a $[-100.0, +100.0]$ scale — **never** a win probability or percentage return expectation.
> - **Zero LLM / ML Generation**: Pure deterministic mathematics and rule-based quantitative methods only.

## 3. Directional Evidence Groups & Sub-Component Caps

To prevent correlated indicators from dominating the score, each sub-component is subject to explicit contribution caps:

### A. Trend Evidence (Weight: 30%)
- **EMA Stack Structure (Max: 40.0 pts)**: Stacked bullish ($EMA_9 > EMA_{21} > EMA_{50} > EMA_{200}$) $+40.0$; partial alignment $\pm 30.0$; stacked bearish $-40.0$.
- **Supertrend Direction (Max: 20.0 pts)**: Bullish boundary active $+20.0$; Bearish boundary $-20.0$.
- **ADX & Directional Movement (Max: 25.0 pts)**: $+DI > -DI$ with $ADX \ge 25.0 \implies +15.0$ to $+25.0$; $-DI > +DI \implies -15.0$ to $-25.0$.
- **24h Rolling VWAP Relationship (Max: 15.0 pts)**: $Close > VWAP \implies +15.0$; $Close < VWAP \implies -15.0$.
- **Normalized Score**: $\text{Sum} \in [-100.0, +100.0]$.

### B. Momentum Evidence (Weight: 20%)
- **Context-Aware RSI (Max: 35.0 pts)**:
  - Strong Bullish Trend + RSI $> 70 \implies +30.0$ (positive momentum continuation, NOT an automatic short).
  - Range / Weak Trend + RSI $> 70 \implies +10.0$ (overextension warning).
  - Strong Bearish Trend + RSI $< 30 \implies -30.0$ (bearish continuation).
  - Baseline RSI $> 52 \implies +20.0$; RSI $< 48 \implies -20.0$.
- **MACD & Histogram (Max: 30.0 pts)**: MACD line $>0$ and Histogram $>0 \implies +30.0$; line $<0$ and Histogram $<0 \implies -30.0$.
- **Stochastic RSI (Max: 20.0 pts)**: $\%K > \%D$ in upper zone $\implies +20.0$; $\%K < \%D$ in lower zone $\implies -20.0$.
- **Rate of Change (Max: 15.0 pts)**: Positive price velocity $>1\% \implies +15.0$; negative $<-1\% \implies -15.0$.

### C. Structure Evidence (Weight: 35%)
- **Swing Sequence Direction (Max: 40.0 pts)**: Confirmed Higher Highs + Higher Lows $+40.0$; Lower Highs + Lower Lows $-40.0$.
- **Break of Structure (BOS) (Max: 35.0 pts)**:
  - Strong Break Quality ($ATR \ge 0.5$, $RVol \ge 1.5$, Body $\ge 0.6$) $\implies \pm 35.0$.
  - Normal Break Quality $\implies \pm 25.0$; Weak Break $\implies \pm 15.0$.
- **Change of Character (CHoCH) (Max: 25.0 pts)**: Bullish CHoCH transition $+25.0$; Bearish CHoCH $-25.0$ (treated as structural transition evidence, not completed reversal).

### D. Volume Evidence (Weight: 15%)
- **Relative Volume (RVol) with Structure (Max: 50.0 pts)**: High volume ($RVol \ge 1.5$) confirming directional structure $\implies \pm 50.0$. High volume without directional structure is classified as neutral activity ($0.0$).
- **On-Balance Volume (OBV) (Max: 50.0 pts)**: Positive accumulation supporting trend $+40.0$; distribution $-40.0$.

## 4. Contextual Modifiers (Volatility & Regime)

Contextual evidence alters setup quality and conviction multiplicatively without creating directional bias:

### A. Volatility Quality Modifier
- `NORMAL` volatility: $1.00\times$
- `LOW` volatility: $0.95\times$
- `VERY_LOW` (Range compression): $0.85\times$
- `HIGH` volatility: $0.85\times$
- `EXTREME` volatility ($ATR\% \ge 88\text{th}$ percentile): $0.60\times$ quality modifier + $15.0$ pt anomaly warning.

### B. Regime Compatibility Modifier
- Aligned / Compatible Regime (`TRENDING_BULLISH` for Long, `TRENDING_BEARISH` for Short): $1.00\times$
- Neutral / Ranging / Transition: $0.85\times$
- Opposing Regime: $0.75\times$
- Uncertain Regime: $0.70\times$

## 5. Conflict Detection & Support/Resistance Constraints

- **Opposing Trend vs Structure**: e.g., Bullish EMAs but Bearish LH/LL structure $\implies$ `HIGH` severity conflict ($25.0$ pt penalty).
- **Sub-Par Breakout Volume**: Bullish BOS with $RVol < 0.8 \implies$ `MEDIUM` severity ($15.0$ pt penalty).
- **Support / Resistance Proximity**:
  - If a potential `LONG_SETUP` has price within $0.25 \times \text{ATR}$ below a strong Resistance zone $\implies$ `HIGH` severity ($25.0$ pt penalty; constrains Long without generating Short).
  - If a potential `SHORT_SETUP` has price within $0.25 \times \text{ATR}$ above a strong Support zone $\implies$ `HIGH` severity ($25.0$ pt penalty; constrains Short without generating Long).
- **Critical Data Invalidation**: Missing candles or data staleness $\implies$ `CRITICAL` severity forcing status `INVALID_DATA`.

## 6. Mathematical Score Traceability

Every signal snapshot includes an explicit `ScoreTrace` object enabling exact mathematical reconstruction:

$$\text{Base Score} = (\text{Trend} \times 0.30) + (\text{Momentum} \times 0.20) + (\text{Structure} \times 0.35) + (\text{Volume} \times 0.15)$$
$$\text{Context Adjusted Score} = \text{Base Score} \times \text{RegimeMod} \times \text{VolMod}$$
$$\text{Net Score} = \text{Context Adjusted Score} - \text{Total Conflict Penalties}$$

---

## 7. Classification Gates & Setup States

- **`LONG_SETUP`**: Net Score $\ge +45.0$, Trend Score $\ge +30.0$, Structure Score $\ge +30.0$, no `HIGH`/`CRITICAL` conflicts, and compatible regime.
- **`SHORT_SETUP`**: Net Score $\le -45.0$, Trend Score $\le -30.0$, Structure Score $\le -30.0$, no `HIGH`/`CRITICAL` conflicts, and compatible regime.
- **`WAIT`**: Directional score existed ($|\text{Base}| \ge 35.0$), but was constrained by S&R proximity, low volume, or moderate conflict.
- **`NEUTRAL`**: Score within neutral band $[-30.0, +30.0]$ with no directional edge.
- **`INVALID_DATA`**: Data quality failed or critical inputs missing.
